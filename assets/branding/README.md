# BatasPH brand assets

The BatasPH emblem combines an open law book, balanced page shapes, an
eight-ray Philippine sun, and a speech-bubble point.

## Palette

- Midnight navy: `#2C3550`
- Warm amber: `#D7A96A`
- Warm ivory: `#F5F1EA`

## Masters and store asset

- `batasph_app_icon_master.png` — original generated opaque master
- `batasph_app_icon_1024.png` — normalized launcher master
- `batasph_logo_mark.png` — original generated transparent mark
- `batasph_logo_mark_1024.png` — normalized transparent mark
- `batasph_play_store_512.png` — 512 × 512 Google Play Store icon

Platform-specific launcher and splash exports are stored in their standard
Android, iOS, and web asset directories.
