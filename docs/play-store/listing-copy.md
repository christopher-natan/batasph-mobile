## Full description

```text
BatasPH is a source-first AI assistant for exploring Philippine law. Ask a question in plain language and get a readable response, with a legal basis when the app can verify one from available sources.

Ask by voice or text. In voice mode, speak naturally and follow the conversation on screen. In text chat, ask follow-up questions and save useful question-and-answer pairs on your device. Choose Auto, English, or Tagalog for the response language.

BatasPH tells you when it cannot verify a reliable answer. Responses are for general information only, may be incomplete or inaccurate, and are not legal advice. For a specific case, rights, or deadlines, consult a qualified Philippine lawyer or the relevant government agency. An internet connection is required for questions and voice conversations.
```

The copy describes implemented features observed in the current app; it avoids promises about account sync, offline answers, or guaranteed legal advice. Review against the final release before publishing.
